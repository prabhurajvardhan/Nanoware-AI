# Product Mission

- **Document ID:** BIBLE-001
- **Status:** DRAFT
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-13

## Purpose
Define the intended product without claiming that proposed capabilities exist today.

## Mission
Enable a small business to register, configure its operating model, publish a safe public presence, accept qualified booking requests, and manage customer conversations and bookings from one tenant-isolated workspace.

## Current versus target

| State | Description |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED current** | Marketing/research site; website audit workflow; Firebase-backed projects and requests; dashboard/admin pages; email helpers. |
| **PROPOSED target** | Self-service business workspaces with public pages, CRM, services, booking, AI assistant, usage metering, subscriptions, and operations tooling. |

## Product outcomes

1. A business owner can reach a usable workspace without operator intervention.
2. A public visitor can discover only explicitly published business information.
3. A booking request is validated, deduplicated, authorized for the relevant tenant, and auditable.
4. Owners see customer and booking state in one dashboard.
5. Usage is attributable to a business and explainable to the owner.
6. Security and tenant isolation are release gates, not post-launch enhancements.

## Non-goals for the initial transformation

- Do not rewrite the existing marketing experience as part of documentation work.
- Do not assume voice is in the first release; it is a later-phase proposal.
- Do not select a billing or AI vendor until a decision is recorded.
