# Subscription and Pricing

- **Document ID:** PROD-005

The supplied roadmap proposes Free and Pro plans plus optional guided setup; these are **commercial assumptions requiring validation**. Before coding, decide currency/tax, trial, grace period, cancellation, refunds, entitlements, provider, invoice ownership, and what counts as a qualified booking request.

Billing state must be derived from verified provider events and reconciled idempotently. Frontend plan labels are presentation only. Usage enforcement must define whether a request is counted at submission, qualification, owner acceptance, or another explicit event.
